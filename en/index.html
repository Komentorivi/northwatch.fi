How it works

It watches, it understands, it tells you.

Once Northwatch is set up in your network, it keeps watch day and night, and you hear from it only when something needs you.

  1. It watches

    Everything that comes in and goes out of your network, and what your devices do inside it. Quietly, around the clock.

  2. It understands

    Thousands of knocks on the door every day are normal, and they are stopped. A device in your network talking to criminals is not. Northwatch knows the difference.

  3. It tells you

    What happened, on which device and what to do, in plain words. You decide. One click and the device is off your network.

A real case

The projector that phoned home.

A cheap Android projector joined a home Wi-Fi on a Sunday evening. Within seconds it started trying to contact a spyware server, twice a second, without pause. The firewall blocked almost all of them. Northwatch noticed the pattern and raised the alarm. The same can happen with a meeting-room projector at the office.

Plain language

An answer, not a wall of codes.

Every alarm explains what happened, how sure Northwatch is and what to do now, soon and later. Written for people, not for experts.
